HUA CHA LI · Est. 2019

Founded in 2019, HUA CHA LI is a chain brand dedicated to premium tea beverages. We source our tea leaves from trusted farms, brew every cup fresh, and customize it to your taste.

Our mission is to bring every customer the most authentic tea experience, filling each sip with surprise and satisfaction. From classic pearl milk tea to refreshing fruit tea, we craft every cup with care — just for you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about HUA CHA LI.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We partner with major food delivery platforms. You can also call us to place an order and pick it up in-store to enjoy it fresh.

Can I customize the sweetness and ice level?

Absolutely. Every drink can be adjusted to your taste — choose your preferred sugar level (0–100%) and ice amount, or go sugar-free.

Do you offer dairy-free or lactose-free options?

Yes, we offer oat milk and soy milk substitutes for most milk tea drinks, so everyone can enjoy a delicious cup.

Where are your stores located?

Our flagship store is at 123 Tea Street, Hong Kong, with more locations opening soon. Follow us for the latest updates.

How can I contact customer service?

Call us at +852 6542 3394, email hello@huachali.com, or use the contact form on this page — we usually reply within 24 hours.
